      How it works

      Each round starts on a red screen. After a random wait of 1.5 to 5 seconds the screen turns green — click or tap as fast as you can. Your time is measured from the moment the screen turns green to the moment you click. Clicking while the screen is still red is a false start: the round is void and you try it again.

      Your rank comes from your average across all rounds: under 180 ms is Pro Gamer, under 220 ms is Lightning, under 270 ms is Sharp, under 320 ms is Average, under 400 ms is Steady, and anything slower is Warming Up. Your best times are saved on this device only, so you can see whether you are improving.

      Honest limits: results vary by device, screen and input. A phone touchscreen, a wireless mouse and a gaming monitor will not give the same numbers, and this test measures your reaction plus your input latency together — it cannot separate the two. Treat the number as a fun benchmark, not a lab measurement.
